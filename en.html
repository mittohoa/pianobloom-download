PianoBloom

Every note
makes a flower bloom.

An Android app that helps children aged 4–12 and parents with no music background learn piano on their own: Bloomie guides every step by voice, with listen – play – create games, no teacher needed.

Learning feels like playing in a music garden

Made for beginners: few words, lots of animation and voice.

🌸

Bloomie leads the way

A rose with headphones greets your child, reads every step aloud (words light up as they are spoken), praises good work and gently hints when something isn't right yet.

🎹

On-screen keyboard or a real piano

A multi-touch virtual keyboard; also USB MIDI, Bluetooth MIDI and the microphone (trial).

🐦🐻

Listening and playing games

"High or low?", Bloomie asks – you answer, flowers bloom with the beat, notes float up from every key your child plays.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parents learn too

A "parent learner" profile with its own progress, tips for parents before and after each lesson, 7/30-day reports with no rankings.

📴

Works fully offline

No internet needed, no ads, no tracking. Microphone sound is processed on the phone only and never sent.

🔒

Parent gate

Settings, reports and privacy controls sit behind a small challenge for grown-ups.

🎵

Songs and rhythm

Follow the glowing keys while Bloomie plays along, or "play in time" with the metronome; tunes your child makes up are saved to replay.

4 gardens, 32 lessons

They open one by one along the journey — each lesson grows a flower, each finished garden grows a big tree; replay any time.

Pack 1 · ages 4–9The sound garden: high/low, steady beat, finding C, C–D–E
Pack 2 · ages 5–9Five happy fingers: C–G five-finger position, up/down, a five-note song
Pack 3 · ages 6–12First music reading: the staff, treble clef, quarter/half notes, rests
Pack 4 · ages 7–12Two hands sing together: left hand, bass clef, two hands, a 4-bar composition

Note: the lessons are drafts not yet reviewed by a piano teacher (clearly marked in the app). Please treat this as a trial. The app is in Vietnamese by default; switch to English in the parent area → Language.

Download and install

The app is not on Google Play yet; install it directly from the APK file.

Download the latest version
  1. Open the link on an Android phone (7.0 or later).
  2. Open the downloaded pianobloom.apk file.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for the browser or Files app.
  4. Open PianoBloom and go to the Parent area to create your child's profile (switch to English under Language).

Older phone (32-bit chip) says "not compatible"? Download the 32-bit version.

Updating: download the new version and install it over the old one — learning progress on the phone is kept.

Using 0.1.6 or older? From 0.1.7 the app has a new id (for Google Play), so Android installs it as a new app next to the old one. To move progress (from 0.1.8): in the old app, Parent area → Privacy → Copy learning data (JSON); then in the new app, Parent area → Privacy → Paste data from the old version. Then you can uninstall the old app.
